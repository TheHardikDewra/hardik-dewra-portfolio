04 · What I check first

Day one of every sprint

Seven things I check on any landing page, before touching the design.

It's the list I run on day one. Take it and run it on your own page, it costs you nothing.

  1. 1

    Can a stranger say what you sell in five seconds?

    If the headline needs the subhead to make sense, the headline isn't doing its job yet.

  2. 2

    Does the headline carry a number or a named outcome?

    "Write 40 ad variants in the time one draft used to take" will always beat "Write faster".

  3. 3

    Is the first button visible on a phone without scrolling?

    Most paid traffic lands on a 390px screen. Check the page there first, not on your monitor.

  4. 4

    Does the button say what happens next?

    "Book a 20-minute call" tells people the cost of clicking. "Get started" tells them nothing.

  5. 5

    Is the proof specific?

    Full names, real companies and real numbers. A quote from "Sarah, CEO" does more harm than no quote at all.

  6. 6

    Are price, risk and time answered before the FAQ?

    People leave at the first unanswered doubt. Put each answer next to the place the doubt shows up.

  7. 7

    Does it load fast on a slow phone?

    A hero image that takes four seconds on mobile data loses people before they read a word.

05 · Process

Five working days

Brief on Monday. Live on Friday.

One page, one conversion goal, five working days. The copy comes first, because a good-looking page with a vague offer still won't sell.

  1. Day 1

    Kickoff and strategy

    You send the brief. I audit your current page, we agree the conversion goal and lock the scope.

  2. Day 2

    Copy first

    Hook, offer and section-by-section copy, drafted in a shared doc before a single pixel moves.

  3. Day 3

    Direction

    Two visual directions and one hero concept. You pick a lane and I run with it.

  4. Day 4

    Full design

    Every section, desktop and mobile, designed in Figma. You see the whole page before it gets built.

  5. Day 5

    Build and ship

    Built in Framer, checked across devices, analytics wired, live on your domain.
